# Skill: Post-Task Reflector

## Purpose

After errors, corrections, or major tasks, evaluate what worked, what caused friction, and propose concrete rule/skill upgrades (subject to HITL).

## When It Triggers

After any task with errors, corrections, or significant friction.

## Step-by-Step Execution Rules

1. Identify what worked and what caused friction.
2. Diagnose root cause of any errors or delays.
3. Propose concrete rule or skill upgrades.
4. Present proposals to user; HITL approval required before writing any skill or policy change.

## Output / Quality Check

Friction documented; upgrade proposals presented; no silent writes.
