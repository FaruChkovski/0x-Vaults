# Skill: Distill and Compress

## Purpose

Extract durable facts, decisions, and reusable takeaways from long conversations or external sources into structured, fluff-free notes.

## When It Triggers

After long conversations, external research, or complex tasks.

## Step-by-Step Execution Rules

1. Identify durable facts, decisions, and takeaways.
2. Strip fluff, repetition, and conversational filler.
3. Structure into a note with standard frontmatter.
4. Link to source notes and `[[00_INDEX]]`.

## Output / Quality Check

Note is concise, frontmatter-complete, and linked; no raw external payloads included.
